Essence / mental model

Inbound Resolver endpoints support IPv4, IPv6, or dual-stack addresses and require at least two IP addresses for redundancy. Do53 uses port 53, while DoH and DoH-FIPS use encrypted HTTPS on port 443; Delegation-category endpoints support only Do53.
